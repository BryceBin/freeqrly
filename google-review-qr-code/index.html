Good to know

Where do I find my Google review link?

In Google Business Profile: open your listing, click Ask for reviews or Share review form, and copy the short link.

Do reviewers need to be signed in?

They need a Google account to post, but scanning works for everyone — the code just opens your review form.

Can I track how many scans it gets?

FreeQrly codes are static and private — no tracking by design. For counts, put a URL shortener with stats in front of the link.

Does the code expire?

No. Static codes never expire and need no account, so your counter card works forever.
