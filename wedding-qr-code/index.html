Good to know

What do couples usually link to?

RSVP forms, shared photo albums (guests upload their shots), digital menus, seating charts, and the gift registry.

Can I match my wedding colors?

Yes — set any ink and paper color. Just keep strong contrast between the code and background so phones read it in dim venues.

How big for table cards?

At least 3x3 cm so guests can scan from across the table without standing up.

Will they still work on the day?

Yes — static codes never expire. Make them months ahead; they will scan on the day and for years after as a keepsake.
