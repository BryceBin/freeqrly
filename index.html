Questions, answered gently

Do free QR codes expire?

Never. The destination lives inside the pattern itself, so a code works as long as its destination does. Paid "dynamic" codes route through a vendor's servers and can stop working when you cancel.

Is my WiFi password safe here?

Yes — it never leaves your browser. For peace of mind, load this page once, disconnect from the internet, and then type.

Static or dynamic — which should I choose?

Static for anything permanent: WiFi cards, contact details, fixed links. Dynamic (editable + scan analytics) suits campaigns, and is on our roadmap.

Can I use these commercially?

Yes. Everything you generate here is yours — packaging, menus, business cards, no attribution required.
