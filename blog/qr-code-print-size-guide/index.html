How big should a printed QR code be?

The short answer: at least 2x2 cm, at 300 dpi, with a clear margin. The longer answer — with the distance rule of thumb printers actually use — is below.

2026-10-14 · 4 min read

The two rules that matter

Rule of thumb 1 — minimum size: 2 x 2 cm (0.8 x 0.8 in). Below that, phone cameras can't resolve the modules reliably, especially codes holding a lot of data like vCards.

Rule of thumb 2 — the 1:10 distance ratio: the code's width should be at least one-tenth of the scanning distance. Scanning from 50 cm (a table card)? 5 cm code. From 5 meters (a poster)? 50 cm code. Printers and signage folks use this ratio as a rule of thumb.

Cheat sheet by use case

Business cards: 2x2 cm minimum — see the business card generator. Keep it clear of the card edge.
Flyers & menus: 3x3 cm. People scan these at arm's length.
Table tents: 4–5 cm, so guests scan from their seats.
Posters: use the 1:10 ratio against expected viewing distance.
Packaging: 2x2 cm minimum, and avoid curved surfaces that distort the pattern.

Resolution: export big

Print needs 300 dpi. A 2 cm code at 300 dpi is only ~240 px — but always export larger and let the designer scale down: PNG at 1024 or 2048 px, or SVG which is infinitely scalable and the best choice for professional print. FreeQrly exports both, free.

Don't forget the quiet zone

Leave a blank margin around the code at least 4 modules wide on every side, as specified in ISO/IEC 18004. Crowding it with text or graphics is one of the top reasons codes fail — more in why your code won't scan.

Logo in the middle? Go bigger

A logo in the center eats into the pattern, so give it room: 3x3 cm minimum in print, and keep the logo under ~30% of the code's area as a rule of thumb.

Sources

  • ISO/IEC 18004:2024 - The QR Code symbology specification. Defines the four error-correction levels (L 7%, M 15%, Q 25%, H 30%) and the 4-module quiet zone.

Good to know

What is the minimum size for a printed QR code?

As a rule of thumb, 2 x 2 cm (0.8 x 0.8 inches). Smaller codes fail on many phones, especially for data-heavy codes like vCards.

How big should a QR code be on a poster?

Use the 1:10 rule of thumb: the code width should be at least one-tenth of the expected scanning distance. A poster scanned from 5 meters needs a 50 cm code.

PNG or SVG for print?

SVG is best — it scales infinitely with no quality loss. If your printer needs raster, export PNG at 2048 px.
