Good to know

Will the code still scan with a logo on it?

Yes. Uploading a logo automatically raises error correction to High, which reserves up to 30% of the pattern for recovery under ISO/IEC 18004 — that is where your logo sits.

What logo file works best?

A square PNG with a transparent background. Avoid fine text inside the logo — bold shapes read better at small sizes.

What size should I print it?

At least 2x2 cm, at 1024 px export or higher. Bigger is safer when a logo covers the center.

Is there a watermark or a fee?

No. Logo upload is free and unlimited, and for static codes your files never leave your browser.
