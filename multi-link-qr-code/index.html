Good to know

What is a multi-link QR code?

A QR code that opens a page full of links instead of a single destination — the classic link-in-bio pattern. One code on your packaging can lead to your shop, your Instagram, and your YouTube at once.

Can I change the links later?

Yes — edit your link page (Linktree, Beacons, or your own page) and the same QR code shows the new links. The code encodes the page URL, not the links themselves.

Linktree or my own page — which is better?

Linktree and Beacons are fastest to set up. Your own page gives you full control over design and no third-party branding. Both work the same way with a QR code.

Does it expire?

No. Static codes never expire and need no account.
