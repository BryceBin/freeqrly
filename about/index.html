What is FreeQrly?

FreeQrly is a free QR code generator with a simple idea: the tool should live in your browser, not on somebody's server. You type, it draws — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or billed.

100% browser-basedEvery code is drawn locally with JavaScript. Your links, contacts, and WiFi passwords never leave your device.
Codes that never expireStatic codes live in the pattern itself; dynamic codes stay editable after printing. No trials, no deactivated codes — what you print today scans in ten years.
Free to startStatic codes need no account at all. Dynamic codes — editable, with scan stats — need a free sign-in. No watermark either way.
10 languagesEnglish, Chinese, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic (RTL), Portuguese, French, Russian, German, Japanese.
Built for printHigh-resolution export and vector SVG, quiet zones respected — made for business cards, menus, and posters.
Agent-friendlyA free API and MCP server so AI assistants can generate codes too.

Questions, answered

Is FreeQrly really free?

Yes — every feature is free, with no watermarks. Static codes are made entirely in your browser and need no account; dynamic codes need a free sign-in so they can be edited later.

Do the QR codes expire?

No — neither kind. A static code's destination lives inside the pattern itself, and dynamic codes stay live and editable for as long as your account exists. Details: do QR codes expire?

Can I use it for my business?

Yes — commercial use is free. Restaurants use it for menus and reviews, shops for review codes and WiFi codes.

Who is behind FreeQrly?

An independent maker who got tired of "free" generators that hold your codes hostage behind a trial. The mascot is Qly, a small elephant who appears across the site.

Is FreeQrly the same as FreeQR?

No. FreeQrly (freeqrly.com) is an independent project and is not affiliated with FreeQR (freeqr.com).
