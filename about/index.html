What is FreeQrly?

FreeQrly is a free QR code generator with a simple idea: static codes should live in your browser, not on somebody's server. You type, it draws — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or billed.

Static codes: 100% browser-basedEvery static code is drawn locally with JavaScript. Your links, contacts, and WiFi passwords never leave your device.
Static codes onlyNo trials, no subscriptions, no deactivated codes. What you print today scans in ten years.
Free, no sign-up for static codesLogo upload, batch generation, 2048 px PNG and SVG export — all free, no watermark. Static codes need no account.
10 languagesEnglish, Chinese, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic (RTL), Portuguese, French, Russian, German, Japanese.
Built for printHigh-resolution export and vector SVG, quiet zones respected — made for business cards, menus, and posters.

Questions, answered

Is FreeQrly really free?

Yes — every feature is free, with no watermarks. Static codes need no sign-up and cost nothing to serve once generated in your browser, so there's nothing to charge for.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. FreeQrly generates static codes only; the destination lives inside the pattern itself. Details: do QR codes expire?

Can I use it for my business?

Yes — commercial use is free. Restaurants use it for menus and reviews, shops for review codes and WiFi codes.

Who is behind FreeQrly?

An independent maker who got tired of "free" generators that hold your codes hostage behind a trial. The mascot is Qly, a small elephant who appears across the site.
