Good to know

Is this scanner private?

Yes. The image is decoded by JavaScript running on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server — load the page, go offline, and image upload still works.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Usually it's too small, low contrast, damaged, or shot at a steep angle. Hold steady, fill the frame with the code, and use good light. The full checklist is in why your QR code won't scan.

Do I need to install anything?

No — any modern mobile or desktop browser works. Camera mode asks for camera permission once; upload mode works everywhere, even offline.

Can I make my own QR codes too?
